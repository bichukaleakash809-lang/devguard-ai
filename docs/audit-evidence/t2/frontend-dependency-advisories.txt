CONCLUSION AND WHY NOTHING WAS CHANGED
--------------------------------------
Real finding, honestly bounded: most of the 18 are unreachable in this app's
configuration, and the reachable remainder is the response-cache class against
a fully static frontend.

The only available fix is next 14 -> 16, a breaking major upgrade across the
App Router. Contract §13.1 forbids dependency changes without explicit
approval, and the standing instruction for this session repeats it. So this is
recorded, triaged and left in place rather than "fixed" by a bump that would
also need the whole frontend re-verified.

Recommendation for the owner: approve `next@^16` as its own phase, with
`npx tsc --noEmit`, `next build`, `next lint` and a manual pass over all five
routes as the acceptance gate. Do not fold it into an unrelated change.
